📖 How to Use FinPro

1
Add Transactions

Click "Add Entry" on the Dashboard or Transactions page. Choose Income or Expense, enter amount, pick a category and date.

2
Set Budgets

Go to Budget page and set monthly limits per category. You'll get alerts when approaching or exceeding limits.

3
Create Goals

Go to Goals and set savings targets with deadlines. Your net profit is tracked toward each goal automatically.

4
Add Recurring Entries

Track monthly subscriptions, EMIs, and regular income on the Recurring page. Hit ✓ to log when paid.

5
Use Tools

Calculate EMIs, estimate taxes, plan SIPs and FDs on the Tools page.

6
Export Reports

Download PDF, Excel, or JSON backups from the Reports page. You can also import data back.
